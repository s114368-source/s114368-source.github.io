arkdown # 🎣 Pygame Fishing Game A simple, addictive 2D fishing minigame built with Python and Pygame. Catch fish, beat your high score, and avoid text glitches with this clean, English-only version! --- ## 🎮 How to Play The gameplay is inspired by classic fishing mechanics (like Stardew Valley): 1. **The Fish (Green)** moves up and down randomly. 2. **The Bobber (Orange)** is controlled by you. 3. **Hold `SPACEBAR`** to make the bobber float up; release it to let gravity pull it down. 4. Keep the green fish **inside** the orange bobber to fill the golden progress bar. 5. If the bar reaches 100%, you catch the fish! If it hits 0%, the fish escapes. --- ## 👑 Features * **High Score System:** Automatically tracks and displays your `BEST` catch size (in cm). * **New Record Alert:** Highlights when you break your historical record. * **Bug-Free Fonts:** Uses standard system fonts to prevent any garbled text or weird characters. --- ## 🚀 Installation & Running ### Prerequisites Make sure you have Python installed on your computer.
